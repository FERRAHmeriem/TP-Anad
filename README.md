# Student Attendance Factors Dataset

## Overview
This dataset provides a detailed representation of factors that influence student attendance in a college environment. It includes a wide variety of variables covering student demographics, lifestyle habits, academic background, environmental conditions, and attendance outcomes.

Each record represents a unique student-day profile with realistic behavior patterns.

## Key Variables
The dataset offers an in-depth view of how different factors collectively impact attendance consistency, including:
* **Academic & Lifestyle:** Study hours, sleep duration, and academic project commitments.
* **Environmental & Logistics:** Travel time, internet access, weather conditions, and hostel residency.
* **Class Context:** Type of class (online/offline) and attendance outcomes.
* **Absenteeism Insights:** Reasons for absence such as illness, personal work, festivals, or travel.

## Real-World Trends Reflected
The data is designed to mirror common trends observed in educational institutions:
* **Academic Engagement:** Students with higher study hours tend to show greater consistency.
* **External Barriers:** Rainy weather or long travel times negatively affect attendance.
* **Mode of Learning:** Online classes increase attendance likelihood due to convenience.
* **Living Conditions:** Hostel students generally have lower travel time and slightly better attendance rates.

## Potential Use Cases
This dataset is suitable for:
* Educational analysis & Institutional planning.
* Behavioral insights & Research.
* Dashboards and attendance trend visualization.
* General statistical exploration and Machine Learning modeling.

> **Note:** Since the data is fully synthetic, it avoids privacy issues while maintaining realistic patterns.
